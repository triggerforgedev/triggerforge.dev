Tower of the Dead privacy policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This policy covers the Tower of the Dead mobile game ("the game"), published by Triggerforge LLC ("we", "us"). Our website has its own privacy policy.

The short version

The game currently has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings (such as volume, camera and display options), which tutorials you have seen, and the address of the last match you joined with a ticket that lets you rejoin it, in the game's own storage on your phone. Uninstalling the game removes them. We never receive them, though Android's own device backup may copy them to your Google account if you have backup turned on.

Online play

Online matches connect your phone to our match server (currently in the United States). To run the match, the server receives your device's IP address, the game commands you issue during the match, a short-lived seat ticket that lets you rejoin if your connection drops, and your device's model name (for example "Galaxy S24"), which other players in the match see in the lobby. This is necessary to play the match with other people.

The server keeps this information only while a match is running and for the short time needed to let you rejoin. It may keep a recording of the match (the commands issued, with no name or contact detail) so that we can replay it, find bugs and verify results. We do not use the IP address to identify you or build a profile.

Information we do not collect

The game does not include third-party analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting software. The game does not collect your advertising ID. Google Play itself may collect information when you download or update the game; see Google's Privacy Policy.

If you email us

We use your email address and anything you write only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list. Ask us and we will delete it.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because we do not link match data to a name or account, we may ask for details such as the approximate time of a match to find it. Email us and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If we add accounts, advertising, purchases, or any other collection of information, we will update this policy before that version of the game is released and change the effective date above.
