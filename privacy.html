Privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026

This policy covers triggerforge.dev, the website of Triggerforge LLC ("we", "us"). Each of our games publishes its own privacy policy, linked from its store listing: Tower of the Dead.

What this website collects

Nothing that we can see. The site has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising, and loads nothing from other websites.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records the IP addresses of visitors to keep the service secure; see the GitHub Pages data collection notice and the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

If you email us

We use your email address and anything you write only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list. Ask us and we will delete it.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Email us and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above.
